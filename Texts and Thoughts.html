Texts And Thoughts

Texts, there are texts;
read when can create waves.
What kind of waves?
All kinds of waves:
Parallel and perpendicular waves,
Calm and violent waves.
Be careful what kind of texts you read.

Thoughts, there are thoughts;
fed with attention can create waves.
What kind of waves?
All kinds of waves:
Parallel and perpendicular waves,
Calm and violent waves.
Be careful what kind of thoughts you feed attention to.



B Dhoonkar

The Thinker


There is a thinker in me,
He likes to think.
He thinks a lot.
I mean really a lot.
I mean really really really a lot.

But he is my friend.
In fact he is my best friend.

You know what,
Now that I think about it,

He is my bestest friend.
In fact, I haven't met anyone like him,
He is the bestest friend I have ever had.

He serves me and I serve the source.

The source for everything,
The source for all the thoughts,

The source for all the words,
Silence...The Beautiful Silence...The Beautiful Void!


B Dhoonkar
